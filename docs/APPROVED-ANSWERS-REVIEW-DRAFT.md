## General Ai Education

### Ai Automation Definition

**Question variations:**
- What is AI automation?
- How can AI automate a business workflow?

### Chatbot Vs Voice Agent

**Question variations:**
- What is the difference between a chatbot and a voice agent?
- How is Voice AI different from a website chatbot?

**Draft answer:**

A chatbot usually interacts through typed messages on a website or messaging channel. A voice agent interacts through spoken conversation, often by phone. The best choice depends on how customers prefer to communicate, the task to complete, the systems involved and the risks of an incorrect response.

AI can misunderstand a question or produce an incorrect or incomplete answer. Important uses need representative testing, clear limits, checks on outputs and a safe path when the system is uncertain. High-impact decisions should not rely only on an unverified AI response.

AI does not automatically mean replacing an employee or an entire role. ### Ai Guardrails Definition

**Status:** `draft`  
**Topic key:** `ai_guardrails_definition`  
**Question variations:**
- What are AI guardrails?
- How can AI be kept within its intended limits?
- What does safer AI behavior mean?

**Draft answer:**

AI guardrails are controls that limit how an AI system is used and how it responds. They can include input checks, approved knowledge, limits on the tasks it may perform, output checks, privacy protections, monitoring and a fallback when a check fails. The controls should match the system’s purpose and risks.

### Ai Suitability

**Status:** `draft`  
**Topic key:** `ai_suitability`  
**Question variations:**
- Is AI right for every business?
- Should every task be automated?
- How do I know if a task is suitable for AI?

**Draft answer:**

Not every task is a good fit for AI. Start with a clear, repeatable problem; consider whether suitable data and system access are available; assess the impact of mistakes; and decide how outputs will be checked. A small, measurable and lower-risk use case can be a more useful starting point than automating everything at once.
